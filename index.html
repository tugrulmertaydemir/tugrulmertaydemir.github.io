Shop Operations Panel

Shop Operations Panel is a web application that helps an online shop owner run their digital-download shops on Etsy. It brings product files, customer messages, orders and monthly finances together in one place, so the owner can deliver the right files to buyers and answer customers on time.

What Shop Operations Panel does

  • Product files: lists the product files the owner keeps in their own Google Drive and checks that each file is complete and that its delivery link opens for buyers.
  • Customer messages: reads the shop's notification e-mails in the owner's Gmail, so new customer messages and help requests are collected in one list and none is missed.
  • Orders and finances: keeps order records, support notes and monthly income and costs together.

How Shop Operations Panel uses Google APIs

  • Google Drive: reads the names, sizes and links of the owner's product files, checks that files open, and, only when the owner asks, sets a delivery folder to "anyone with the link can view". It never deletes or moves files.
  • Gmail (read-only): reads the e-mails the shop platform sends (customer-message and order notices). It never sends, changes or deletes e-mail.
  • Shop Operations Panel does not create, edit or generate images, video or any other media.
  • Google user data is never used to create AI-generated imagery of any kind, including non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII), and is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Shop Operations Panel complies with the Google APIs Terms of Service and the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who uses it

Shop Operations Panel is used by the shop owner and the team members they add, to manage their own shops and their own Google accounts. This page and the privacy policy are public and can be read by anyone without signing in.
