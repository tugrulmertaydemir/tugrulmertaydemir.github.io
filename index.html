Shop Operations Panel

A private, self-hosted tool that the shop owner uses to run their own online digital-download shops.

What it does

  • Lists the owner's product files in their own Google Drive and checks that delivery links work for customers.
  • Reads the shop's notification e-mails in the owner's own Gmail (read-only) so customer messages can be answered on time.
  • Keeps orders, customer support notes and monthly finances in one place.

Who can use it

Only the shop owner and the people they personally add. The panel runs on the owner's own computer; it is not a public service, there is no sign-up, and it is not offered to anyone else.

Google data

The panel connects only to Google accounts that belong to the shop owner. Read how Google data is used and protected in the privacy policy.
