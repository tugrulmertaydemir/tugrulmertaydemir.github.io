Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Shop Operations Panel ("the panel") is a private tool that a shop owner runs on their own computer to manage their own online shops. It is not a public service. This policy explains what the panel does with Google user data.

Google permissions and why they are needed

  • Google Drive (drive): to list the owner's product folders and files (names, sizes, links), to check that delivery links open for customers, to set a delivery folder to "anyone with the link can view" when the owner asks for it, and to download a product file temporarily for a quality check (is the archive complete and does it open). The panel does not delete or move files in Drive.
  • Gmail (gmail.readonly): to read the shop's notification e-mails (for example new customer messages and order notices) so the owner can answer customers. Read-only: the panel never sends, changes or deletes e-mail.

Where the data is kept

  • All data stays on the owner's own computer, in the panel's local database. There is no cloud server of ours.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored encrypted. Files downloaded for a quality check are deleted as soon as the check ends.
  • Only the owner and the people they personally add can open the panel.

Sharing

  • Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.
  • When the owner asks the panel to draft or translate a reply to a customer message, the text of that message is sent to the AI provider the owner has set up (Anthropic's Claude API) only to produce the draft. No other Google data is sent.

The panel's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Access can be removed at any time in the panel (Settings → Google → Remove) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Removing an account in the panel deletes its tokens and the Drive and Gmail data the panel stored for it.
