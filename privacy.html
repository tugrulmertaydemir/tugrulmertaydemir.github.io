Shop Operations Panel – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Shop Operations Panel ("the app", "we") is a web application that helps an online shop owner manage their Etsy digital-download shops: product files, customer messages, orders and finances. This policy explains which data the app collects, how it is used, where it is stored, who it is shared with, and how it can be deleted. It covers all data the app receives from Google APIs.

1. Data we access from Google

The app asks for access only after the account owner signs in with Google and approves it.

  • Google account e-mail address (openid, email): to show which Google account is connected.
  • Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive): file and folder names, sizes, types, links and sharing settings of the owner's product folders. File contents are downloaded only temporarily, to check that a product file is complete and opens.
  • Gmail (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly): sender, subject, date and text of e-mails sent by the shop platform (Etsy), such as new customer messages, help requests and order notices.

2. How we use this data

  • Drive data is used only to list product files, check that they are complete, check that delivery links open for buyers, and, when the owner asks, set a delivery folder to "anyone with the link can view". The app never deletes or moves Drive files.
  • Gmail data is used only to collect customer messages and order notices in one list so the owner can answer customers on time, and to show reminders about unanswered messages. The app never sends, changes or deletes e-mail.
  • Google data is used only to provide these features to the owner. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to build user profiles, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Google data is never used to create, edit or generate images or other media, including AI-generated imagery.

3. Data we collect that is not from Google

  • Sign-in details of the app's users (name, e-mail, and a securely hashed password).
  • Shop data the owner enters or imports: orders, customer names, support notes, costs and income.
  • A log of important actions (for example sign-ins and settings changes) for security.

4. Storage and security

  • All data is stored in the app's own database on the server the owner runs. We do not keep a copy on any other server.
  • Google access tokens are stored encrypted. Traffic to Google uses HTTPS.
  • Files downloaded for a check are deleted as soon as the check ends.
  • Only the owner and the team members they add can sign in; two-factor authentication is available.

5. Sharing and transfer

  • We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with third parties.
  • One exception, only when the owner asks for it: to draft or translate a reply to a customer message, the text of that one message is sent to the AI provider the owner has set up (Anthropic's Claude API), only to produce the draft. That provider does not use it to train models. No other Google data is sent.
  • We may disclose data only if required by law.

Shop Operations Panel's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention and deletion

  • E-mail texts of order, payment and Etsy notices are cleared after 90 days; customer messages are kept as long as the owner needs them for support.
  • Access can be removed at any time in the app (Settings → Google → Remove) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Removing a Google account in the app deletes its tokens and the Drive and Gmail data stored for it.
  • To ask for deletion of any other data, write to the contact address below; we answer within 30 days.

7. Children

The app is a business tool and is not meant for children under 16.
